Kern County holds more Basque dining rooms than any county in the United States. The rest of the state runs from a members' club in South San Francisco to a tasting counter in West Hollywood.

Why Bakersfieldcited

Basque men came to the southern San Joaquin to run sheep, and the boarding houses that fed them clustered by the Bakersfield rail yard on Sumner and East 19th. The houses outlasted the trade. Two of them still serve on the same streets.

The spreadinference

A room in Chino keeps a monthly boarders' table. A club in South San Francisco opened its dining room to anyone. A counter in West Hollywood files itself under Basque and Catalan. Same state, four different answers to what a Basque restaurant is.
