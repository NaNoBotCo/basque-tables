Baserri

The Basque farmhouse: house, barn, byre and family under one roof, and the unit of inheritance that sent younger sons to America.

Root
baso + herri — wild place, settlement (Euskara)tradition

Note

Primogeniture kept the baserri whole and gave the rest of the children a ticket. That is the mechanism behind every room in this directory.
