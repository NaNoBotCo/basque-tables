A word we have not got

kuadrilla

kwa-DREE-ya

literally: the squad

The group of friends you fell in with at fifteen and are still seeing every week at sixty. You do not join one and you do not leave one.

The reason a Basque bar at seven o'clock is full of people who already know what everyone is having.

Room

Ogi Deli, Elko

Elko, 460 Commercial Street. Bread all day, and on Thursday and Friday nights the only pintxo-pote in this directory — a pintxo and a pint for $5.

Where
460 Commercial Street, Elko, NV
Seating
A counter and tables. On pintxo nights the counter is the point: you stand, you eat off a skewer, and on Fridays there is a card game going.
Week
MTWTFSS
Deli Mon–Fri 8am–3pm · Bar & Pintxos Thu and Fri 4:30pm–10pm · pintxo-pote 4:30–6
Dinner
$5 Pintxo-pote — a pintxo and a pint, a glass of house wine, or a glass of sagardoa
