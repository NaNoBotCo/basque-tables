Santa Fe Basque Restaurant

Fresno, on Maroa. A small family house that keeps two standing dates: a chicken-liver dinner the second Thursday of the month, and a Basque breakfast.

Now

Noon to seven Monday and Tuesday, noon to eight Wednesday to Saturday, shut Sunday. Reservations help them staff the night.

At the table

The chicken-liver dinner comes as soup, salad, potato salad with shrimp, chicken livers with gravy, fried chicken, wine and dessert. The Basque breakfast is eggs, bacon, chorizo, biscuits and gravy, potatoes, pancakes, cheese and wine.
