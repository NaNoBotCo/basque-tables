Now

The Basque Cultural Center is a members' club; its restaurant, now called Zazpi, serves the public lunch and dinner six days a week.

Note

Zazpi is seven in Euskara — the seven Basque provinces, the same count in the Zazpiak Bat motto.

A word we have not got

hamaiketako

ah-mai-ke-TA-ko

literally: the eleven-o'clock

A second breakfast with a proper name. Around eleven, work stops: a sandwich, often chorizo, and a glass of wine. Not a snack. An institution.

English gets "elevenses" and a biscuit. Euskara gets a sandwich and a drink.
