Now

Communal long tables, dishes passed down the row. The house calls the Picon Punch a mandatory beginning to a Basque dinner.

Before

The J.T. in the name is the initials of the owners who put it up in 1955. The Lekumberrys bought it on 1 April 1960. In the 1960s lunch was $1.25 and dinner $1.50.

A word we have not got

amatxi / amona

a-MA-chee / a-MO-na

literally: grandmother, north / grandmother, south

Same woman, two sides of a border. Amatxi in Iparralde, the French side; amona in Hegoalde, the Spanish side.

Which one a Bakersfield family uses tells you which valley they left.
